== T20 WORLD CUP ==
The first ICC Men's T20 World Cup was held in 2007 in South Africa. India won it by beating Pakistan in the final.

== SACHIN TENDULKAR ==
Nickname: Master Blaster. Country: India.
Test runs: 15,921. ODI runs: 18,426.
International centuries: 100 (51 in Tests and 49 in ODIs). He is the only player to score 100 international centuries.
Total international runs: 34,357.

== DON BRADMAN ==
Country: Australia. Played 52 Tests and scored 6,996 runs.
Test batting average: 99.94, the highest in Test history.

== BRIAN LARA ==
Country: West Indies.
In 2004 he scored 400 not out against England in an Antigua Test, the highest individual Test score.

== BOWLING RECORDS ==
Muttiah Muralitharan (Sri Lanka): 800 Test wickets (the most ever) and 534 ODI wickets.
Shane Warne (Australia): 708 Test wickets.

== ROHIT SHARMA ==
Country: India. Highest ODI score: 264 against Sri Lanka in 2014, the highest individual score in ODIs.

== BASIC RULES ==
A Test match is played over up to five days with two innings per side.
An ODI has 50 overs per side. A T20 match has 20 overs per side.
An over consists of six legal deliveries.
A "duck" means a batter is dismissed without scoring.
A "century" is 100 or more runs scored by a batter in one innings.
